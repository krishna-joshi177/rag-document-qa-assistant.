Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 24 days of paid leave per calendar year.
Unused leave of up to 10 days can be carried forward to the next year.

Work From Home Policy
Hybrid employees must work from office at least 3 days per week.
WFH requests beyond this must be approved by the reporting manager.

Certification Reimbursement
Employees can claim 100% reimbursement for approved cloud certifications
(AWS, Azure, GCP) after passing the exam. Claims must be raised within 30 days
with the payment receipt and the certificate attached.
